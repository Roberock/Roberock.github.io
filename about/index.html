About Roberto Rocchetta

Research at the intersection of reliability, learning and energy.

I work with uncertainty as a central part of engineering decisions, from infrastructure planning to lifetime prediction.

Biography

Method, evidence and real systems.

Dr Roberto Rocchetta is a Research Associate and Lecturer in the Energy Systems Team at the Institute for Applied Sustainability to the Built Environment (ISAAC), Department of Environment, Construction and Design (DACD), SUPSI. His interdisciplinary research combines network resilience, reliability engineering, statistical learning and energy-system modelling.

He holds a PhD in Reliability Engineering and System Safety and an MRes in Decision Making under Risk and Uncertainty from the University of Liverpool, as well as MSc and BSc degrees in Energy Engineering from the University of Bologna.

His work develops machine learning and artificial intelligence for reliability, availability, maintainability and safety (RAMS); uncertainty quantification; stochastic and robust optimisation; probabilistic risk assessment; and reinforcement learning for energy and critical infrastructure. The methods have been applied to power-grid outage and maintenance planning, renewable integration and storage, electric mobility, prognostics and structural health monitoring.

Areas of expertise

What guides the research

01 / Quantify

Uncertainty & risk

Scenario optimisation with statistical reliability guarantees, Bayesian and imprecise-probability updating, and rare-event analysis.

02 / Learn

Models & policies

Statistical learning and reinforcement learning for sequential decisions in complex, networked systems.

03 / Apply

Infrastructure decisions

Interpretable, statistically defensible tools for operation, maintenance and resilience.

Teaching & collaboration

Across disciplines and institutions.

Roberto teaches Decision Making under Uncertainty at SUPSI and has taught Survival and Reliability Analysis at Eindhoven University of Technology. He supervises master’s research on risk-informed power-system planning.

His research collaborations include Swissgrid, Mobility, NASA Langley Research Center and the National Institute of Aerospace, Philips, ETH Zurich, Signify and Politecnico di Milano. His current project portfolio includes PEDAID3 (SNSF–DUT, 2026–2028), a 2026 research mandate from the University of Hamburg on reinforcement learning for networked infrastructure inspection and maintenance, and PROPER-Grids (Swiss Federal Office of Energy, 2023–2027).
